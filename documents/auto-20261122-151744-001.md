Notice of Cybersecurity Incident. Please read this entire letter.

Dear Parent or Guardian of Gracie Sutliff:

Central Ohio Primary Care Physicians, Inc. ("COPC," "we," or "us") was the victim of a cybersecurity incident that may have
affected the security of your minor dependent's personal information. You are receiving this letter because your minor dependent's
personal information may have been impacted during the cybersecurity incident. This letter will provide you with information
about the incident, steps we are taking in response, and steps you may take to guard against identity theft and fraud, should you
feel it is appropriate to do so.

What happened?

On August 25, 2026, COPC discovered that we had experienced a cybersecurity incident resulting from a social-engineering, or
"vishing" (voice phishing), attack (the "Incident"). Through this attack, a criminal threat actor was able to gain unauthorized access
to certain files containing protected health information. Immediately upon discovery of the Incident, COPC activated its incident-
response protocols and engaged cybersecurity and legal experts. COPC has been working closely with these experts to investigate
the Incident and comply with all data privacy regulations and requirements. The Incident was discovered expeditiously, and
security measures were implemented to contain the Incident and mitigate any potential impact.

What information was involved?

The information potentially exposed may have included protected health information, which could include sensitive personal
information such as first and last name, address, Social Security Number, limited health related patient data including patient ID
number, financial information, and other potential identifying information.

To date, COPC is unaware of any misuse (or attempted misuse) of your minor dependent's personal information for identity theft
or fraud stemming from the Incident.

Please note that MyChart (Epic) was not breached as part of this Incident and patients with MyChart access can continue
to log in to their portal and connect with their providers as usual.

What are we doing?

Upon learning of the Incident, COPC immediately launched an investigation and took measures to stop the cyber attack, including
taking impacted systems offline. COPC is currently working with law enforcement in their criminal investigation of the attack.
COPC will also continue to evaluate additional steps that may be taken and implement additional security measures to make
its computer systems even stronger than before.
